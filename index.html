Why I'm doing this

Seven years ago I was the one refreshing mock results at midnight.

I prepped for CAT alongside a full-time job, failed my first attempt, rebuilt my approach from scratch, and scored 99.67%ile the second time round — calls from IIM B, IIM C, IIM L, IIM K, FMS, and XLRI.

Most of what slowed me down wasn't the syllabus. It was not knowing what to prioritise, second-guessing my sectional strategy, and not having anyone to sanity-check my plan. That's the gap I'm trying to close for the people I mentor now.

— Kartik Singhal
